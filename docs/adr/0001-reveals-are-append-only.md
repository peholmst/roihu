# Reveals are append-only

A crew member cannot unsee an inject they have read, so the system does not pretend
otherwise: a reveal is never withdrawn, erased or edited. The timeline is therefore
the exercise's only state — what a crew member can see is exactly the reveals aimed
at their position.

## Considered Options

Retraction, recorded as its own event, was the alternative: the officer withdraws
access after a mis-tap, the inject disappears from the phone, and the timeline shows
both the reveal and the retraction. It was rejected because the timeline is the
evidence the debrief rests on, and because it would model an untruth — the crew
member may already have read the inject.

## Consequences

There is no recovery from a mis-reveal, so the reveal control must be hard to hit
by accident. An officer who reveals the wrong inject corrects it over the radio, and
the slip stays in the timeline where the debrief can discuss it.
